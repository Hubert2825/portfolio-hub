Music

Music is what I care about most. I arrange, produce, and write songs, and I write orchestral scores.

How I write

I used to think writing orchestral music was about getting the right notes. Now I think it's about meaning. A score starts with a theme, but a theme is more than a melody. It's an idea I'm trying to get across.

From there, every decision has to earn its place. I listen to each note and ask whether it belongs, whether it carries weight, and whether it says something.

  • Dynamics shape emotion. A crescendo builds tension, and a sudden drop makes space.
  • Articulation and phrasing give a line its character. They decide whether it breathes, pushes, or holds back.
  • Orchestration is where it comes alive. Every instrument is a voice, and the job is to make them speak as one.

The biggest lesson so far is that depth doesn't come from adding more. It comes from being intentional with what's already there.
