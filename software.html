How my stack changed

I started with native PHP, HTML, and CSS. After I accidentally lost project files, I stopped rebuilding what I'd lost and rebuilt how I work. I moved to React, Node.js, Laravel, and SQL Server, and to strict version control with GitHub.

Frameworks gave me built-in authentication, routing, and database migrations, and reusable components made the interfaces cleaner. Building my own APIs showed me why they matter.
